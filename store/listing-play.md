### Full description – EN (≤ 4000)
```
Yassarna is a Mushaf app for reading, understanding and memorising the Quran. It works offline, has no ads and needs no account. The interface is Arabic, with an English option.

The Mushaf
• Three printed Mushaf editions: the current King Fahd Complex Madinah Mushaf, the older Madinah print, and a Shamarly-layout Mushaf. You download the edition you choose once, then it works offline.
• A clear, zoomable text view with colour-coded tajweed rules.
• Page turning like a book or vertical reading with auto-scroll, and two pages side by side on tablets.
• Night mode and several page colours.

Understanding
• Four tafsirs: Ibn Kathir, al-Tabari, al-Qurtubi and al-Saadi.
• Grammatical analysis (i'rab), reasons of revelation, the ten qira'at and word meanings from classical dictionaries.
• Similar verses (mutashabihat) for memorisers, and an English translation of the meanings.
• Search the Quran by a word or part of a verse; the verse is highlighted on the page.

Listening and memorisation
• Recitation by several reciters with verse repeat; audio is kept on the phone after the first play.
• Memorisation mode that hides verses, with a memorisation and review map.
• Voice review: an optional on-device recitation recogniser follows your recitation without internet and points out a wrong word. It is a study aid and can make mistakes.
• Tajweed lessons.

Every day
• A khatma plan over the number of days you choose, plus page and verse bookmarks.
• Morning and evening adhkar and a tasbih counter.
• Prayer times with an alert at adhan time (phone alarm tone), Qibla direction and a Ramadan timetable.
• "Verse of the day" and "Next prayer" home-screen widgets.

Privacy
The app collects no personal data and has no ads or trackers. Your location stays on the device and is used only for the Qibla and prayer times. Donating short clips of your recitation to improve the recogniser is optional, off by default, and you can delete what you sent at any time.

The Quran text is letter-identical to the Madinah Mushaf (Hafs from Asim) as published by the King Fahd Complex. All tafsir and book sources are credited in the app under About. This is an independent project, not affiliated with any official body.
```

## 4. Notes for the reviewer (App content › App access › instructions, or the release "notes for review" if shown)
```
No login is needed. The app is an offline Quran reader (Arabic UI; English under Settings › Language).
- Page images of the Mushaf editions and the optional on-device recitation-recognition model are DATA files the user chooses to
  download from https://github.com/drmalek1987/quran-karim/releases. They contain no executable code: the native ONNX Runtime
  libraries are packaged in the app bundle (lib/arm64-v8a) and are never downloaded.
- Microphone: only when the user taps the mic in the memorisation test (Settings shows the model terms first). Optional voice donation is
  off by default, needs explicit consent in a dialog, and "Delete all my recordings" is in Settings.
- Exact alarms: only for the prayer-time alerts the user turns on (Prayer tab › bell icon).
- Location: foreground only, for Qibla and prayer times, never sent.
```
