# 5.11+ plan — user patch of 2026-11-06 (+ open items from the 5.10 phone test)

Goal check: every group serves either the recognition goal (>95% for any reciter, any place) or the near-complete app goal.
At home recognition is already ~98% (a bit slower than 5.7), so the biggest gaps are **following/timing on the page** and **noise / low voice outdoors**.

## Classification
- **BUG**: wrong behaviour of something that exists.
- **UX**: wording, placement, flow.
- **FEAT**: new feature.
- **ML**: model / training.
- **QA-INFRA**: test setup.

## Groups — easiest to hardest

### G1. Settings wording and placement (UX, easy)
1. Settings: write "wifi فقط" instead of "واي فاي بس".
2. Settings: remove the model picker. Put a "download the recitation model" option in its place, and keep NVIDIA as the only recogniser. The phone SpeechRecognizer caused the 5.9 confusion.
3. Move the "delete my data" button: make it small and put it inside the privacy policy, not in the general settings.
4. Privacy policy: add the model terms of use, including the use of voice to improve the app.

### G2. Swipe margins (BUG, 5.10 #5 FAIL)
5. Margins still show during horizontal flipping. The 5.10 per-page clip did not fix it.
   - First record the swipe on the real phone: `adb shell screenrecord`, or scrcpy `--record`, then pull frames with ffmpeg.
   - Find which layer shows the margin: the curl or the slide, pages below `cropFrom`, the spread view, or the fullscreen crop vars.
   - Fix it, then confirm on the phone frames, not only in headless Chromium.

### G3. Onboarding and consent (UX/FEAT, easy–medium)
6. Welcome screen: a Skip button on every step.
7. Welcome screen: a step to download the recitation model.
8. First model download: show the usage policy with an "I agree" box. Agreeing is required before the model works, and the consent is stored.
9. First mic use: a tips message (quiet place, check the mic, recite clearly). It also offers a mic test that measures the level, the noise floor and the SNR, and gives a quality rating.

### G4. "More" menu (UX, medium)
10. Reorganise the More menu: group related items, give it a clear order and better layout.

### G5. One statistics section (FEAT, medium)
11. Put reading stats, recitation stats and voice-upload stats in one Stats section, with an attractive visual design (charts and tiles, Arabic RTL).

### G6. Hifz flow redesign (UX/FEAT, medium–hard)
12. The memorisation test becomes the main function. The memorisation map and the memorisation tracking serve it, with direct links between the three.
13. Map: one box per surah that is shorter than one mushaf page.
14. Map: an option to choose the ayah. When the mic opens, jump to the start of the page, auto-scroll with the recitation, then close at the end or go to the next page if auto-next is on.
15. Hifz page: an "auto flip" option that turns to the next page by itself.

### G7. Recitation following and timing engine (BUG/FEAT, hard — biggest user-felt gap)
These items share one cause: the page follower and its timing (fin-only decisions, matcher window, ASR chunking, view lifecycle).

16. Flip **immediately** after the last word printed on the page (5.10 #2). Decide on partial results; do not wait for `fin` plus 700 ms.
17. The first ayah of the next page is recognised slowly (5.10 #4).
18. Sometimes it does not notice that reading moved to the next page, and keeps scoring wrongly until the mic is restarted or the page is turned. Needs re-sync and jump detection into the next page.
19. The text is recognised correctly but the line opens late, or never opens.
20. After several sentences recognition slows down and stalls until the reader goes back and repeats. Suspect growing buffers, a growing CTC window, or a growing `disp` or `V`. Profile it.
21. The written text lags behind the recitation pace. Measure the latency per chunk and find where the time goes.
22. Landscape: recognition often stops. Suspect the rotation or a re-route killing the mic or the ASR session.
23. Landscape: the page follows what is heard and flips automatically, using the chosen flip mode.
24. Recitation in scroll mode should work like hide mode: all ayat on all pages hidden, each line or ayah revealed when it is recited, with auto-scroll.
25. Mic on the main screen: locate the ayah being read, jump to it, and continue on the mushaf pages.
26. Note: about 98% at home, slightly slower than 5.7. Compare the 5.7 and 5.10 pipeline latency.

### G8. Voice data upload (FEAT, hard, privacy)
27. Auto-save segments while reading (not only during the hifz test), with an optional auto-upload to the database. Apply the same quality rules as the hifz-test uploads so the data is usable for training: alignment confidence, SNR, length, Uthmani labels and consent.
28. Long recitation sessions are uploaded too (from the earlier BACKLOG P-B 7). This needs the backend decision from the existing voice-donation pipeline.

### G9. Multi-device QA (QA-INFRA, medium — set it up early, then use it for every later group)
29. Test on 4 screen classes: phone small, phone large, foldable and tablet. Every QA item runs on each one.
    - Use API 35 AVDs, because the API 37 ones are unstable here: Phone_API35, plus new Small/Fold/Tablet API35.
    - Use Maestro flows per device, plus a ui.js viewport matrix.

### G10. Noise, low voice and speed robustness (ML, hardest, GPU)
30. Outdoors with a low voice: slower, and sometimes whole ayat are missed. Do the L3 noise retrain: MUSAN and street noise, low gain, reverb. Also tune VAD and AGC on the device.
31. Train for fast reading (up to about 4 levels or about 1.5x, hadr) and slow mujawwad reading. Use speed perturbation (0.7–1.5) and mujawwad reciters in the data. Follow the P3 lessons.

## Order of work
G1 → G2 → G9 (setup only) → G3 → G4 → G5 → G6 → G7 → G8 → G10.

G7 is the most important for the user. It comes after the quick wins so that the multi-device QA exists first.
